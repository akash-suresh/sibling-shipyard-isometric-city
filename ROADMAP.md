# Isometric City Roadmap

This roadmap tracks all the major architectural upgrades, features, and polish we have planned for the city. 

## 🏗️ Architecture & Buildings
- `[ ]` **Coach HQ Overhaul**: Upgrade the generic tower into a massive bespoke corporate skyscraper. Potential features: glass elevators, rooftop helipad, and a detailed entrance plaza.
- `[ ]` **3 of Spades Studio**: Overhaul the generic studio building into a slick, modern design agency or a cozy brick-and-mortar storefront.
- `[ ]` **Shipyard Expansion**: Add interior details to the Shipyard, such as scaffolding inside the truss roof, dry-dock mechanics, or construction sparks.

## 🚢 River & Vehicles
- `[ ]` **Dynamic Ships**: Build procedural Cargo Ships or Victorian Steamboats that spawn at the Shipyard, sail down the canal, pass under the open Amsterdam drawbridge, and exit the city.
- `[ ]` **Railroad System**: Add a train track crossing the city with a moving locomotive.

## 🌳 Ambient Life & Environment
- `[ ]` **City Parks**: Replace empty grass tiles with dedicated park areas featuring benches, fountains, and trees.
- `[ ]` **Advanced Traffic**: Expand the ambient vehicle system with intersection logic or specialized vehicles (e.g., food trucks, double-decker buses).
- `[ ]` **Day/Night Cycle**: Implement dynamic lighting that transitions the city from day to night, turning on building lights, streetlamps, and neon signs automatically.
